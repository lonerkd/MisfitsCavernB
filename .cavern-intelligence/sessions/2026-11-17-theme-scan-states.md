# Session - 2026-11-17: the theme scan learns what opens on a click (BACKLOG 3.35)

A route scan reads a page as it loads, so the job modal's unreadable submit button (white on pale violet, found by the route scan only on the page's own button) stayed hidden in its modal. `scripts/theme-scan.mjs` now has **`--states`**: a short list, `STATES`, of click-opened surfaces. Each loads its route, does what a person does (open the modal and type a title; open the notifications panel, search, the island, or on a phone the menu sheet), waits 1.5 s for the open animation to settle (measured mid-fade the modal's button looked disabled and the label overlapped the text, which was only the transition), then measures with the same contrast code as a route (refactored into one `MEASURE`, no behaviour change for routes). A control that is not there is a failure, never a silent pass; the notifications panel is desktop-only (the phone has Updates, a link, not a panel).

**Run on the local stack and a production build:** Paper, Neon and Paper on a phone each report "every piece of text reads" for all states. The check has teeth: at a 12:1 threshold every state reports many text kinds. **The job modal's submit button, the item left open by #641, is verified:** measured in a browser, 1.99 → 9.65 in Neon and Misfit and 2.50 → 7.90 in Paper (Paper had the same fault the other way round: dark ink on the deep violet). Left in 3.35: toasts, confirm dialogs, empty and error states and other modals (add each to `STATES`); retaking `bible/screens`. Not seen on a real iPhone.
