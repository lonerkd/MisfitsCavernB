# Session — 2026-11-02: Ctrl Z outside the editor

`lib/os/undo.ts` is a short per-tool stack of real inverses, and Ctrl/⌘ Z outside a text box runs the newest one for the tool you are in (`components/shell/UndoKeys.tsx`, mounted in the shell and in split panes), with a toast that says what was undone or why it could not be. Registered in the Studio's schedule: moving a scene to another day, changing its status, and auto-schedule. Nothing is registered for what cannot be taken back, so Undo never claims more than it can do; with nothing to undo the key is left to the browser.

**Chord change:** Undo/Redo moved from the editor's own chords to global ones (`undo`, `redo`, `redo-y`): in a text box the box's own history answers (the editor's handler is unchanged in behaviour), elsewhere the suite's stack does. The shortcuts panel now lists Undo in its Global group.

**Verified:** unit tests for the stack (order, per-tool scope, async, failure, size, subscribers) and the chord table; `e2e/studio-undo.spec.ts` in a real browser (Alt+→ moves a scene to day 2, Ctrl Z puts it back on day 1 and says so; a status change and its undo; nothing left to undo shows no toast); the editor undo and chord specs still pass; typecheck, lint, full unit suite, bundle budget. No migration.

**Left (BACKLOG 4.1):** redo outside text boxes, more registered actions (rename a location, budget edits, tasks), the island showing the pending undo (the O slot), an undo that survives a reload.

**Found in review (Copilot, six findings, all real) and fixed:** (1) an undo wrote the old value back unconditionally, so it could overwrite a newer move from another device or a later action: the inverses now use one conditional write (`updateSceneIf`: the scene must still hold what the action left it with; persona-tested in `tests/integration/scene-update-if.test.ts`) and refuse with a message otherwise (`e2e/studio-undo.spec.ts` changes the scene behind the page's back and sees "Could not undo … changed since", the newer day kept); auto-schedule's undo puts back the scenes it can and says how many were changed since. (2) The stack outlived a sign-out: `resetOS` now clears it. (3) The Studio switching project kept the old project's undo eligible: entries carry a context (the Studio sets it to the active project) and other contexts are hidden. (4) The tool and project were read after the awaited write: `undoOrigin()` is captured before the first await and handed to `pushUndo`. (5) Two quick presses raced their writes: undos now run one at a time, newest first. (6) The 50-entry cap was global: it is per tool.

**Noted, not caused here:** `e2e/editor-undo.spec.ts` failed once in four runs of the combined set on this build (once on a value check, once on `getByLabel('Script', { exact: true })` matching two elements), and passed alone and in other runs. That spec was not touched; the second element labelled "Script" is the thing to look at (BACKLOG S).

Auto-schedule's undo, one write per scene, now keeps the scenes that failed in a fresh retryable undo and leaves alone the ones changed since, saying both. `clearUndo` no longer wipes where the person is working (only the actions), and the Studio clears its context when it unmounts. The text-box guard also covers `role=textbox/searchbox/combobox`. The "changed since" message now also says "or you can no longer edit it". **Declined, with reasons:** the activity feed keeps "wrapped scene 4" after an undo (it is the log of what happened, not state; an undo does not rewrite history); status restores the raw previous value (the unknown-status case the finding raised is restored as it was, only a null becomes "planned", which is how the board already reads it); "Redo, in a text box" in the shortcuts panel is a true label of what exists, and redo outside text boxes is in the backlog.
