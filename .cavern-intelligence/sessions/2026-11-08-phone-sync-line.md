# Session — 2026-11-08: a stuck save is visible while you type on a phone (from the review)

The editor's status bar (title, DRAFT, sprint, the offline-queue chip) is hidden while the phone keyboard is up, to give the page its room, so an offline queue or a server that could not be reached was invisible exactly while the person typed. `SyncStatusChip.tsx` now has `PhoneSyncLine`: the same chip body (the queue's words, its retry countdown and its "Sync now" button) in a one-line strip fixed above the tool strip, **rendered only when the queue has something to say** (offline, waiting, syncing, can't reach the server), so on a healthy connection nothing changes. The chip's hook and body were pulled out (`useSyncView`, `ChipBody`) so the desktop chip and the phone line cannot drift apart. **Tests:** 3 in `SyncStatusChip.test.tsx` (nothing when healthy; a stuck save shows above the strip with its button; offline shows with nothing queued). **Verified:** the phone editor and keyboard browser specs still pass on a fresh build. **Not verified:** the strip on a real iPhone, and its stacking against the tool strip in landscape (offset 48 px there).
