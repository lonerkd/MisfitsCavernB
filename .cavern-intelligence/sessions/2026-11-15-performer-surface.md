# Session - 2026-11-15: the performer surface (BACKLOG 3.51, item 1)

**The gap (from the role suites audit):** performers (Actor, Stunt performer, Voice actor) were the first of the two Thin roles. Their only surface was the crew call page, reachable only from a notification: no list of their calls, and the sides the production prints from the call sheet panel were not readable by the person who has to learn them.

**Built (no migration):** (1) **`/call` is My calls**: every issued call sheet on the productions the person is on, with their own call (or, for a role they play, that call; else the general call), upcoming first and earlier below, and whether they have confirmed the version (`studio.listMyCalls`, read through RLS; drafts are not listed). (2) **Your pages** on the call sheet, for anyone cast as a character: their scenes of that day with their own cue, parentheticals and speeches marked, how many speeches, and a Print button (`lib/film/my-sides.ts` over the existing `buildSides`); the script is read and parsed on demand, so the call page's weight is unchanged. Scenes are matched to the parsed script by heading, trusting a scene row's ordinal only when it points at the same heading, because the page parses without the project's cast and places and can number scenes differently from the rows the Studio wrote. (3) **Getting there**: Your day's On set card opens a person's own call (`/call/[id]`; the owner still goes to the Studio's schedule) and links to All my calls; the Production tab's note for a performer links to My calls.

**Tests:** unit `my-sides.test.ts` (8: heading keys, ordinal trusted only when the heading agrees, a repeated heading used once, a scene not in the script, sides in shooting order with the right lines marked, none for someone not cast, one entry per character played); persona `tests/integration/my-calls.test.ts` (6: a performer sees the issued sheet with their own call, not the general one; a draft is left out; the confirmed version shows; crew with no call of their own get none; an outsider sees nothing; a production they are not on does not appear); browser `e2e/performer-calls.spec.ts` (My calls, the call sheet, their pages with their lines marked, confirm, the Production tab's link, an outsider; axe clean) and the existing call-sheet, community and view-as specs. All 101 integration files and the unit suite pass; lint, build and the page budgets pass.

**Found by the accessibility check:** the call rows' `aria-label` did not contain their visible text; the label is dropped and the content names the link. **Left (BACKLOG 3.51):** a lines-learning or self-tape tool, anything after the shoot day (R19); `/call` in the mobile audit's route list; the outside roles (the other Thin role). **No migration, so nothing to apply to production or staging.**
