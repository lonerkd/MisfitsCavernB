# Session — 2026-11-07: the cavern visualizer, Soundtrack search first, previews, share popdown, landscape safe areas

**Visualizer.** The player's open panel has a full-screen button (top right); it opens `CavernVisualizer`, a generative cave (stalactites, rising spores, a breathing glow) over the whole screen, with the song, transport and a seek bar that fade after a few still seconds; Esc, Space (play/pause) and a close button; real fullscreen where the browser has it (not iOS Safari: the overlay covers the screen anyway); reduced motion draws one still frame. It follows playback (alive while playing, dim and still when paused). **It is not beat-synced**: Spotify gives the page no audio signal (the playback stream is protected and the audio-analysis endpoints are closed to new apps), so nothing pretends to be a spectrum; the component says so. Styling is a CSS module (the cavern is always dark, whatever the theme).
**Soundtrack.** Search is the first tab and the one that opens; once Spotify is connected and a project is in view the script's mood fills the box and the tracks load with nothing to press (`generateContextualSearchQuery`; "Refresh from the script" does it again; the old "Magic Context Fill" button is gone). Result cards show Play and Save on touch (they were hover-only). **Previews:** each result, and each suggestion in the editor's Sound tab, has a Preview button that opens Spotify's own embedded player under the row (`SpotifyPreview`); Spotify no longer gives apps clip links in search results, so the embed is the in-app way. It plays a clip when the listener is not signed in to Spotify in the browser and the song when they are, and is separate from the suite's player.
**Editor rail.** The Script tab is now called Insights (Scene and Script read too alike). **Share.** The editor's Share popdown is fixed to the screen's top right (the header scrolls sideways on a phone and clipped it).
**Landscape on iPhone.** With `viewport-fit=cover`, a phone on its side drew under the Dynamic Island and the home indicator and the island sat on the last lines of every page: under 500 px tall on a touch screen the page now keeps `env(safe-area-inset-left/right)`, leaves room for the island at the foot, the island dock sits above the home indicator and clears the side insets, and the editor's keyboard-mode container respects them. **Not verified on a real iPhone** (Chromium has no safe areas); no browser run of the visualizer or the new Soundtrack flow. **Left:** Soundtrack as the home of the player and all sound (library, SFX, tags, table read, visualizer in one place) is a design to plan with the owner.
