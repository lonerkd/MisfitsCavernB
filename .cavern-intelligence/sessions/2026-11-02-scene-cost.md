# Session — 2026-11-02: what a scene costs, and what a shoot day costs

The first slice of the money work (BACKLOG 4.6): the Money tab now answers "what does each scene cost, and each day?" from data the suite already holds (the breakdown's elements, their tags in scenes, and the shoot days).

**The logic** (`lib/studio/scene-cost.ts`, pure, all arithmetic in whole cents): an element costs the figure someone entered, or its category's unit cost as a default. An element used in several scenes (one prop in three scenes) is **paid once**, so its cost is split between those scenes (the odd cent goes to the first), and a scene's cost is its own elements plus its share of the shared ones. That makes the scenes, and the days, add up to the project's total with nothing counted twice: the original slice text said "a day's cost the sum of its scenes", which would have charged a shared prop once per scene. A day is the sum of the scenes scheduled on it; scenes with no day come last. The table says how much of a figure rests on a default estimate and how many elements have no price anywhere (so the total is low), and the footer says how much sits on elements tagged in no scene.

**Verified:** 7 unit tests (entered figure versus default; a shared element split with the cents adding up exactly; days summing to the project total; estimate and unpriced counts; an element in no scene; tags for scenes that are gone; strings and blanks from the database; a tag listed twice); a real browser (`e2e/scene-cost.spec.ts`: a lamp used in two scenes, an apron on a $40 default, two shoot days: Day 1 shows $90, Day 2 $50, "incl. $50 shared", "$40 is a default estimate", total $140); I looked at the rendered table. No migration. **Not verified:** a big project (hundreds of elements); the same figure on the stripboard and the scene cards (left in BACKLOG 4.6).
