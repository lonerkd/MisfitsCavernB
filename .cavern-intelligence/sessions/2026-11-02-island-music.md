# Session — 2026-11-02: the island holds the music that is playing

Where the previous slice had to move a call out of a page first, this one did not: Spotify's player already lives in a suite-wide provider (`SpotifyProvider` in the suite layout), so what was playing was already known on every page. The island now holds it: the track's name, the artists, a progress bar, and **Pause / Play** and **Next** (`lib/island/musicActivity.ts`, pure and tested; `useMusicActivity` registers it from the island, so it shows on every page). Music ranks lowest of the activities, so a call, an upload, a sprint or a waiting queue shows first and the music is one tap behind the "+N".

**Two decisions that are not obvious.** (1) A paused song is **let go after two minutes**: holding it for the rest of the session would replace the island's resting pill (where you are, the page's lead number) for anyone who had ever played something. Playing keeps it; a session that has never played anything holds nothing, even signed in to Spotify. (2) The bar moves in whole percent: the player reports progress every second, and a finer bar would republish the island several times a second for nothing a person can see.

**Looked at first and not done:** recording. Capture's recorder lives inside a modal sheet on the phone, and lifting it into the suite (as the call was) would be a large refactor for something a person does deliberately, on one screen. Uploads with progress need upload progress events the Supabase client does not give (XHR or TUS), a separate piece of work.

**Verified:** 7 tests for the wording, controls and the bar; 4 for the hook (never-played holds nothing, playing holds, a just-paused track stays and is let go after the hold, playing again keeps it); typecheck, lint, 1929 unit tests, build and bundle budget clean; the island specs that already exist (activities, call, island, layout) all pass in a real browser with the hook mounted (7 of 7). **Not verified:** a real Spotify Premium account (the Web Playback SDK needs a signed-in Premium user and cannot be driven in CI: no mocks ship in the product), so how it looks and behaves with live playback is unchecked. The component it renders into (label, bar, buttons) is the one already looked at for the sprint, the queue and the call.
