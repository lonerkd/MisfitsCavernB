# Session — 2026-11-03: Paper is the default theme

The owner chose Paper (the light look) as the default, then the rest in their order. `DEFAULT_THEME_ID = 'paper'` (`lib/themes/themes.ts`): a person who has not chosen sees Paper (the page's first paint, `DEFAULT_CHOICE`, the picker's initial card), Paper is first in the picker and the rest follow in their old order (Cavern, Vampire, Blueprint, …). Someone who picked Cavern keeps it (their stored choice is untouched); System still gives Paper on a light device and Cavern on a dark one; the public share pages stay Cavern (a shared page looks the way its maker designed it). The browser's `theme-color` and the PWA manifest's colours follow Paper (`#f6f1e7`).

**Found by looking (the reason a default needs eyes, not only tests):** Paper had always been selectable, but three places were only ever right in dark themes, and showed at once as the default: the **editor's writing stage** was a hard-coded `#050505` (near-black) under text that follows the theme, so Paper's dark ink was invisible on black: the stage is now `var(--bg)`; the **Studio's undated day header** was white on the theme's pale surface (`.skyPlain` title now uses the theme's text colour); and the **stripboard's date box** was white text on translucent black over a light panel (now theme tokens). Looked at as a new visitor: sign-in, Today, Studio schedule, editor, Lounge, and the editor and schedule again in Cavern and Slate (unchanged).

**Verified:** unit tests for the default, the order and the fallbacks (`lib/themes/themes.test.ts`); typecheck, lint, the full unit suite; the screenshots above. **Not done:** only those five screens were walked in Paper; the rest of the suite should be (BACKLOG, S): a light-theme pass over every screen (`npm run bible:shots` under Paper).

**Found in review (Copilot) and fixed:** the early script (the one that paints before anything is drawn) applied any stored theme id as it was, so a malformed or unknown id set a `data-theme` with no CSS block and the first frame used the base dark tokens until the initializer ran. It now checks the id against the catalogue and falls back to the default (tested by running the script against a stand-in page: a known id is kept, an unknown one becomes Paper, System follows the device, nothing stored leaves the page as rendered). **Declined (nit):** the System card sits before the Paper preset in the picker; System and Custom are modes beside the presets, and Paper is first among the presets.
