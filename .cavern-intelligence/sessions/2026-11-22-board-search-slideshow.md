# Session - 2026-11-22: search within a mood board, and a slideshow (BACKLOG 4.5)

No migration, no new data path.

**Search.** A field above the board's canvas (`/` jumps to it from anywhere in the boards unless the person is typing; Enter / Shift+Enter or the arrow buttons step through the matches, each one scrolled to and focused; Esc or Clear empties it). A card matches when every word of the query is on it: a note's text, an item's title, its notes (where an Openverse credit is kept), its tag (`media.board`), its source's host and its kind; case and accents do not matter. Matches are highlighted, the other cards are hidden while a search is on, a count says how many, and a search with nothing says so. A match found only through its notes or tag shows that line on the card, so it is clear why it matched. Pure logic and tests: `lib/studio/board-search.ts`, `board-search.test.ts`. I did not take Ctrl+F: it is the browser's find and a board can be longer than the canvas.

**Slideshow.** `components/boards/BoardSlideshow.tsx`, loaded on first open (`next/dynamic`), used by the Studio board and by the shared page (`/b/<token>`). One slide per card in reading order (`lib/studio/board-slides.ts`); images and notes shown, video / audio / PDF / link items as a title with an Open link. Arrows and swipe move, Space plays and pauses (3 / 5 / 10 s, off by default, stops on the last slide), Esc closes and focus returns to the button that opened it; focus is held inside; a live region announces "3 of 12: title"; no animation under reduced motion; safe areas via `--mc-safe-top`, `--mc-rail` and `env()`. Rendered in a portal on the body (inside the Studio it was drawn under the page's own bars). It starts at the selected card. The pitch deck's presenter is inline in `PitchDeck.tsx` and not reusable, so this is its own small component.

**The shared page** builds its slides from what `get_shared_board` already returns and the signed URLs the page already made (`sharedSlides`); the item's notes are not in that payload, so a visitor sees no credit lines. Nothing was widened. Also fixed there: the canvas was `role="img"`, which hid the cards from a screen reader; it is a labelled group now.
