# Session - 2026-11-19: the coverage tracker on set (BACKLOG 3.18, graph step 6)

On set (Production › On set) now says, once the day is rolling, whether it will get its shots: **shots got per hour of shooting** (lunch taken out), **how many more the day can get before the planned wrap at that pace**, **how many are short**, and which scenes to **protect** (those whose remaining shots fit in what the pace can still get, in running order) and which are **at risk**. It forecasts only from what the day has done: with fewer than two shots got or under half an hour rolling it says "too early to forecast" and nothing else; with no planned wrap, or once past it, or when every shot is got or dropped, it says nothing. Wrapped scenes and dropped shots are left out. `coverage` and `shootingMinutes` (`lib/studio/coverage.ts`, pure) feed one `role="status"` line in `OnSetView`.

**Decision (mine):** "protect" is running order, not value: the suite does not know which shots matter most, and guessing would be a made-up feature. The line gives the 1st AD the numbers and the running-order consequence, and the decision to drop, move or push stays theirs. Priorities on shots (must-have / nice-to-have) would make it smarter; that is a product choice for the owner.

**Verified:** typecheck, lint, unit tests (`lib/studio/coverage.test.ts`: lunch taken out, waiting, done, too early, on pace, behind with the protected and at-risk scenes, wrapped scenes, past wrap), and `e2e/coverage.spec.ts` (new: rolling two hours, four shots got, a little over two hours to wrap: "behind … 2 shots an hour … 4 short … Protect scene 2. At risk: scene 3.") plus `e2e/on-set.spec.ts` on a production build on the local stack. The spec is skipped between 21:00 and 04:00 (its times are built around now). **Not verified:** a phone on a real set; the offline copy of the day (the line is computed from the same rows, so it shows from the copy too, but that was not exercised).
