# 2026-11-26 — The deck fits the phone

Five screenshots in four minutes, one complaint: the island's deck was
composed at desktop width, and on a phone its parts collide. The
notifications panel, the project list and the sound player all opened
anchored to their buttons and ran off the left edge. The project
button's name pill stood on the icon row after a tap. Turned on its
side, the phone was handed the full desktop dock, and its strip and
Continue line ran off the edges.

The same round caught the bell in the top bar: it is the island's bell,
built to rise, so in the top bar its panel rose off the top of the
screen — on desktop too. The bell now takes a placement: the island's
rises, the top bar's drops.

Now, on the compact surface, each panel spans the screen and floats
just above the island — the island measures the room over itself
(--island-room), so the fit holds at any island size. Name pills are
a pointer courtesy and no longer render on the compact dock. A
landscape phone (coarse pointer, short viewport) gives any dock the
compact manners: the strip wraps, key badges and hints go, long names
truncate. The frosted glass is untouched — the owner likes the
transparency.

No database changes. The owner's phone is the proof.
