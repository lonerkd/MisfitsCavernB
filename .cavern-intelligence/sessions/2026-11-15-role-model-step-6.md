# Session - 2026-11-15: the role model, step 6 (view as a role, a suite per craft, and the audit)

**The ask:** "make sure each role's full suite is built and their tools are well made and interconnected". It widened this step beyond view-as: the owner needed to *see* each role's suite and to know where it falls short, with evidence.

**Built (no migration):** (1) **View as a role**: while previewing as Crew the owner picks a craft and an access level (`setViewAsRole` / `useViewAsRole`); `useMyCapabilities` answers for that role, so Money, purchase orders, claims, paperwork, the casting board and the hub's budget panel show what that role sees; the banner says what it can and cannot do; the database still treats the owner as the owner. (2) **A suite per craft** (`lib/roles/toolkit.ts`): the ordered Production tools each of the 58 crafts works in, a department fallback for crafts an admin adds, a home for roles outside the Studio, and their gaps. The Production tab opens with **Your tools** for a person with a craft (and, while previewing, a note of what the role still lacks); the Your role card on Your day uses the same suites; tool names have one source (`PRODUCTION_VIEW_LABELS`); the old four-shortcut helper (`toolsFor`) is gone. (3) **The interconnection check** (`role-toolkits.test.ts`, against the real catalogue and templates). (4) **The audit**: [plans/2026-11-15-role-suites-audit.md](../plans/2026-11-15-role-suites-audit.md), with the ranked gaps as BACKLOG 3.51.

**What the check found on its first run:** ten roles held a permission without the tool that uses it (Producer, Line producer and Production manager: breakdown and locations; Coordinator: locations; Director: casting; Location manager and Music supervisor: paperwork). All fixed. **A mistake of mine, caught before it shipped:** I had written that call-sheet acknowledgements are not recorded; they are (the issue bar shows who has acknowledged), so the Coordinator's gap now says the flow is unrun, not missing. The audit is limited to what the repository can show.

**Tests:** unit `toolkit.test.ts` (6), `viewAs.test.ts` (role store), `core.test.ts`; integration `role-toolkits.test.ts` (3) and the whole integration suite; browser `e2e/view-as-role.spec.ts` (preview as an accountant, a casting director and a gaffer, with the suite, the screens and the gaps for each) and the existing `view-as`, role, invitation, crew-craft and accessibility specs (the old view-as spec's banner wording changed). Lint, build and the page budgets pass.

**Not verified:** any tool's quality for a real practitioner (that needs the practitioner protocol); the phone, offline and print behaviour of each suite; the surfaces outside the Production tab. **No migration, so nothing to apply to production or staging.**
