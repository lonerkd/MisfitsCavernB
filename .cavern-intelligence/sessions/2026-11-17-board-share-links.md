# Session - 2026-11-17: a mood board's share link

A person who shapes the project (owner, lead or contributor) can make a link to one mood board from Studio > Library > Mood boards > Share link: `/b/<token>`, readable by anyone holding it (the cards, the notes and the images on that board, nothing else), unlisted. Copy, New link (the old address stops at once) and Stop sharing (closes it at once). This is the "presentation link" Cineboard leads with, and it was the first thing left on the boards list.
