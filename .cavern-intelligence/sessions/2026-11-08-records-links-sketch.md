# Session — 2026-11-08: a sketch for linking the role tools to the film graph

Documentation only. Nine of the 17 production role tools hold a scene as typed text and two (Costumes, Makeup) a character as typed text, while the film graph already has the real rows. [The plan](../plans/2026-11-08-records-linked-to-the-graph.md): two columns on `production_records` (`scene_ids uuid[]`, `character_id uuid` with `ON DELETE SET NULL`), integrity through triggers (a scene must be of the record's own project; a deleted scene leaves the arrays), the typed text kept as a label and never as the truth, one shared scene / character picker, a backfill that matches text to rows and leaves the ambiguous as labels, then the scene and entity cards listing a scene's records. Two decisions for the owner are in the plan (one scene or several; whether a scene not yet written may be a label-only record). Nothing built, no code changed.
