# Session — 2026-11-08: the portfolio, step 2: privacy that is real

The audit's worst finding: the three portfolio tables were readable by anyone, signed in or not, by a direct query, so the share token on each piece protected nothing (anyone could list every piece and its token), and there was no private or unlisted piece. **Migration `20261108020000_portfolio_visibility`:** `portfolio_projects.visibility` (`private` by default, `unlisted`, `public`; existing pieces set to public, since they were already readable by everyone and nothing shown should disappear); the three `using true` policies replaced by one `SELECT` policy each (a piece: public, or yours; its media and blocks: readable when their piece is); **`get_shared_portfolio(token)`**, a definer function (on the §2.D allowlist with its gate) that serves a public or unlisted piece by its exact token with its media, blocks and author, and nothing for a private one or an unknown token; `get_recent_work` and `get_person_credits` now list or link only public pieces (an owner still sees their own). **App:** the public piece page and its link preview read through the function; the manage screen has a "Who can see it" choice (Private, Link only, Public, with what each means) when creating a piece and on every piece, and the copy-link button is off for a private one; the pitch board no longer creates a shareable link on its own: it starts private and has a **Publish a link** button (link only); a piece added from a credit is private until published. **Tests:** `tests/integration/portfolio-visibility.test.ts` (11: new pieces are private; the owner reads all; crew, an outsider and anon read only the public piece, its media and blocks, even by id, and cannot find an unlisted or private token by listing; nobody else changes visibility; only three states; the share function opens public and unlisted pieces for anyone and nothing for a private one or an unknown token, and closes again when made private; `get_recent_work` lists only public pieces), the older advisor test now says a *public* piece is read by everyone, 4 new unit tests for the data functions, 3 for the states, and the preview tests rewritten for the function. The whole integration suite (83 files) and unit suite pass. **Not verified:** the screens by eye (signed in) or on a phone. **Left:** a `visibility` choice on the pitch board beyond one button, the cover on the public page, the handle and project pages (steps 3 and 5), and a directory opt-out wording once the handle page exists.
