# Session - 2026-11-17: two homes, one rule (BACKLOG 3.36)

**The owner's question:** "community tab was supposed to be the home screen for mobile, while the landing site is the regular home page, the routing is kinda confusing... post sign-in there's no way to get to the actual home page unless u manually edit the url." What the code did: `/` is the public landing and never redirects (a signed-in person saw it with the button "Enter Studio"); `/community` is the signed-in home (the installed app's `start_url`, the first tab, where sign-in lands); nothing in the signed-in shell linked back to `/`, and several "Home" links pointed at the wrong one (the editor's back arrow, the admin pages, the in-suite error pages, the Spotify callback's "Return to Hub", a non-admin turned away from `/admin`, all to `/`).

**Decided with the owner (option A with a part of C):** `/` stays the public front page for everyone, signed in or out, with no redirect (shared links, previews and search keep working). It is **reachable when signed in**: a *Front page* entry in the island dock beside Profile and Settings, in the phone's menu sheet, and in the command palette. The landing's signed-in button reads **Open Community** (it said Enter Studio and went to Projects). The installed app already starts at Community, so the phone needed no redirect. Inside the signed-in suite, every "Home" now means Community (editor back arrow, `/admin` pages and error boundary, the Projects and Lounge error pages, the Spotify callback, the `/admin` bounce in `proxy.ts`); public and signed-out pages (share links, 404, auth errors) keep `/`. The rule is written in `routing-and-surface.md`.

**Verified:** typecheck; the shell, mobile, app and editor-header unit tests; `e2e/home-routing.spec.ts` on the local stack and a production build (3 of 3): signed in, the dock's Front page opens `/`, "Open Community" goes back; on a touch phone the menu sheet's Front page opens `/`; a signed-out visitor still gets the landing with "Enter The Cavern". **Not done:** Settings has no Front page link (the dock, the phone menu and the palette cover it); a signed-in visit to the bare domain on a phone browser (not the installed app) still shows the landing, by decision, with its Open Community button at the top; not seen on a real iPhone.
