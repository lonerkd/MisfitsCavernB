# Session - 2026-11-14: the role model, step 3a (the budget, who is cast and the paperwork follow the role)

**Found by looking first:** `budget_items` had one policy for everything on `can_access_project`, so a view-only member (and a pending or declined invitee) could read and **change the budget**; any confirmed member, view-only included, could recast or remove castings; paperwork was already limited to people who can shape the project. On production the blast radius of tightening this is small (24 crew rows across 26 projects, all confirmed, 23 with a craft; no producers or accountants among them: the owners run the money).

**Built** (migration `20261114000100`): budget lines, spend and vendors read with `budget.read` and write with `budget.write`; hours are your own, or everyone's with `budget.write`; castings are read by the whole crew (call sheets and continuity use them) and written with `casting.decide`; paperwork and its storage bucket read with `paperwork.read` (or a document about you) and write with `paperwork.write`. The owner holds every capability, so nothing changes for them. The screens say what is going on instead of looking empty: the hub's budget panel and Studio › Money (no budget / read-only / run it), Paperwork, and the casting board's buttons.

**A mistake the persona test caught in my own first draft:** I let `budget.read` show everyone's hours; a production designer holds `budget.read` for their own spend, so they would have seen everyone's hours (payroll). Reading others' hours now needs `budget.write`, like approving them.

**Tests:** persona `tests/integration/protected-by-capability.test.ts` (11: owner and outsider; a department head reads but cannot change or write spend and vendors; a role with no budget sees none; an accountant runs all of it; a view-only accountant cannot write even when granted (this used to be open); the owner's grant and removal; hours; who is cast; paperwork and the files); `money` and `documents` persona tests now give Jordan the craft that does that work (the old tests assumed any crew member could); browser `e2e/role-scoped-money.spec.ts`; all 90 integration files and the unit suite pass.
