# Session - 2026-11-15: the role model, step 5 (why-denied, and asking the owner)

**The gap:** step 3 made the budget, casting decisions and paperwork follow the role, and the screens said "held by the people who run the money. Ask the project owner": a dead end, because there was nothing to press. Someone who needed the budget had to leave the suite and send a message.

**Built** (migration `20261115000100`): `request_capability(project, capability)`, a definer function on the allowlist. A confirmed member asks for one named permission; the project's owner gets a notification (who asked, in which craft, for what) that links to Studio › Cast & crew, where the owner already switches a person's rights. It grants nothing and adds no table. One open request per person and permission, so a second press while the first is unread does nothing (it cannot flood the owner); asking as the owner, or for a permission already held, does nothing; an outsider, someone only invited, an unknown permission and a signed-out caller are refused. In the app, one component, `NeedsCapability`, says what is held back, which permission it needs in the catalogue's own words, and offers **Ask the owner** (it says "Asked" after, and says so when it could not send); it replaces the dead-end notes in the hub's budget panel, Studio › Money (hidden and read-only), purchase orders, insurance claims, paperwork and the casting board.
