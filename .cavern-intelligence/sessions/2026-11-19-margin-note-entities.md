# Session - 2026-11-19: a margin note is a thing in the graph (BACKLOG 3.18 d, editor rung E5)

**Migration `20261119000200`:** `entities.kind` gains `note`; `internal.entity_sync()` (the existing definer function, same signature, body extended) maintains an entity under a margin note's own id for the plain types (note, revision, reference), with triggers on `script_annotations` (insert, text change, delete); existing notes are backfilled. A shot, beat or to-do note already becomes its own thing (a shot, a beat, a task) and gets no entity. Visibility is unchanged in effect: the annotation's readers are the project's creator and members, who are the people who read entities.

**App:** `EntityKind` gains `note` (card, mention picker, group titles). When a plain note is written in the editor, `addAnnotation` ties it to its scene with an `about` link **by scene id**, so the note follows the scene through rewrites and the scene's card lists it; a failed link never loses the note. The note opens its own card (its text, the scene it is about, anything attached).

**Decision (mine):** E6 is closed without a contraction: the measurement of 2026-11-17 showed the remaining text columns are trigger inputs or printed labels, so "stop writing them from the parse and drop them" would remove something the graph reads. Recorded in `backlog-detail.md` 3.18.

**Verified:** typecheck, persona tests (`tests/integration/margin-note-entities.test.ts`: a note, a revision and a reference become entities and a to-do does not; a changed text renames the entity; delete removes the entity and its links; Jordan (crew) sees it and links it, Riley (outsider) and anon see nothing), the full integration suite, and `e2e/margin-note-card.spec.ts` (a note written in the editor gets its entity and its `about` link to the scene, the scene's card lists it, the note's own card names the scene) on a production build on the local stack. **Not verified:** a phone; mentions inside the note text. **After merge (RULES 5):** apply the migration to production, then staging, then the drift check.
