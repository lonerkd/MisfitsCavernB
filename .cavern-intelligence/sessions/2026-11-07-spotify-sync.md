# Session — 2026-11-07: Spotify, in step with what is already playing (sound plan, step 2)

The owner: the Spotify experience is not designed for working — no sync with the song already playing. Until now the suite only knew its own Web Playback SDK device, so music running on the phone or the desktop app was invisible and a song picked in the suite started a second player. `lib/spotify/playback.ts` reads `GET /me/player` (`parsePlayback`: the track or episode, position, the device and its volume; null for an ad or nothing) and sends controls (`controlRequest`: play, pause, next, previous, seek, volume, transfer; pure, tested). `SpotifyContext` polls every 4 seconds while the page is visible (and at once when it becomes visible), backs off for a 429's Retry-After, and when the music is on a device that is not this browser's player it puts that song into the shared state (`currentTrack`, `isPlaying`, progress, duration, volume) and exposes `elsewhere` (the device). Controls drive that device through the API; `playUri` targets it; `playHere()` transfers playback to this browser; `seek()` is new. Because the island's music activity and the global player read the same state, they follow without change. The Sound tab shows "Playing on Pixel 8. These controls drive it.", a seek bar and "Play here". **Tests:** 11 for the playback module, 6 for the provider's sync (shows the remote song, drives that device, a chosen song goes there, nothing playing clears it, Play here, the in-app player being active is not "elsewhere"), 3 more for the panel. **Limits:** Spotify only lets Premium accounts be controlled; a free account sees but cannot drive; verified with fakes, not a real account. **Left:** scene tags (step 3), showing the device in the global player.
