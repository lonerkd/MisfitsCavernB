# Session - 2026-11-15: the director's day at a glance (BACKLOG 3.51 item 4, R11)

**The gap:** the director's suite listed their tools, but nothing told them, on the day's page, what the next shoot day held and what still needed them; they had to open Schedule, Shots and the call sheet to find out. The audit's R11.

**Built (no migration):** a **The day at a glance** card on Community › Your day for a person with a Direction craft (Director, First or Second assistant director) on a production, and for the owner of a production whose own profile craft is one of those. For the next dated shoot day (today or later): the scenes on it in order with location, cast and shots planned for each; the crew call and weather; and a short **needs you** list: nothing scheduled yet, the call sheet not issued (only when the day is within a week, louder on the day itself), how many scenes have no shots, how many of the confirmed crew have not acknowledged the issued version (the same count the Studio's issue bar uses). Each scene says what it lacks (no shots, no location, no cast). It links to the Shot list and the Schedule. `lib/today/director.ts` is the pure summary (tested), `getDirectorDay` in `lib/supabase/today.ts` reads through the person's own access (no new policy, function or table).

**Tests:** unit `director.test.ts` (6); browser `e2e/director-day.spec.ts` (3: the director sees the day with its scenes, one shot planned, the call sheet not issued and the scene with no shots, axe clean; a gaffer and an outsider see no card) and the existing community, today-owner, crew-invitation and phone specs. **Not done:** a live on-the-day view apart from On set; an owner who directs but whose profile craft is not a Direction craft does not see the card; the number of days ahead is one (the next).
