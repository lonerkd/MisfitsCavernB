# Session — 2026-11-08: fixes from the Claude review of the sound and phone work

A `code-review` pass (medium) over the day's merged work found real bugs; the ones that were small and certain are fixed here, with tests. **The visualizer closed itself:** its fullscreen and key effect depended on `onClose` and `togglePlay`, which change identity whenever playback does (and the parent passes an inline `onClose`); each re-run left fullscreen, whose `fullscreenchange` called `onClose`. The handlers now read the latest callbacks through a ref and the effect runs once. **Space fired twice** on a focused visualizer button (the window handler and the button's own activation): Space now plays or pauses only when no control has focus. **The playback poll never backed off** on a 401/403/network error (it retried every 4 s on every page): failures now wait 8 s, 16 s … at most a minute (Spotify's own wait when it gives one), and success resets. **A stopped remote song stayed on screen** (`currentTrack`/`isPlaying` kept the other device's last song when `elsewhere` cleared): it is cleared when the music that came from another device stops. **"Tag this song" silently moved a track** off another scene (a track has one scene): the toast now says "Moved to Scene n (it was tagged to another scene)". **Tests:** 2 for the visualizer (re-render does not close it and the latest callback runs; Space on a focused control), 2 for the provider (the stopped song is cleared; the poll backs off). **Not fixed, in BACKLOG 3.42:** remote controls ignoring a refusal, a null-id remote device, the broad `audio refs update` policy, the editor status bar hidden while typing in portrait. #464 (the CI audit) needs the owner to post the `claude-review` approval or merge: posting it from the session was refused as self-approval, which is the intended guard.
