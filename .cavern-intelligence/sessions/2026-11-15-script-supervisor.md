# Session - 2026-11-15: the script supervisor's lined script and selected takes (BACKLOG 3.51 item 4, R12)

**The gap:** the Script supervisor tool logged scene, take, slate, lens, notes and a circle as free text. Nothing tied a take to a planned shot or to the lines it covered, so there was no lined script, circling several takes of one shot was allowed, and the editor had no list of which take to use. The audit's R12.

**Built (no migration; the records are `scriptNotes` in `production_records`, whose data is free-form jsonb):** (1) a take now links to a planned shot of its scene and records the run of lines it covers (from, to) and whether those lines are off camera. (2) **Lined script:** pick a scene and see its script lines numbered, with a bar down the side for each take that covers them (solid when on camera, dotted off camera, bold and warm when circled), and a line saying which lines no selected take covers ("Not covered by a selected take: lines 4–7"). Lines are the scene's own, without blanks or the heading, read from the script through the same parser the call page uses. (3) **One selected take per shot:** circling a take (or logging one circled) un-circles the other takes of the same shot of the same scene; takes with no shot link circle freely. (4) **Takes for the editor:** the circled takes by scene and shot, with a shot that has takes but none circled shown as a gap; the print sheet gains a Lines column. Pure logic in `lib/studio/lined-script.ts` and `lib/film/lining-lines.ts`.

**Tests:** unit `lined-script.test.ts` (8), `lining-lines.test.ts` (3), a print test for the lines column; browser `e2e/script-supervisor.spec.ts` (log takes, exclusivity per shot, the lined bars, the uncovered lines then full coverage, the editor list, a reload, axe clean) plus `crew-records` and the 70 settings-view unit tests (their Studio mock gained `shots`). **Not done / not verified:** continuity photos on a take, take timings, the selected takes arriving in the editor's timeline or the editorial handoff, paper-style page breaks; a script supervisor has not used it (the practitioner protocol). `/studio` is 379.9 of 384 kB.
