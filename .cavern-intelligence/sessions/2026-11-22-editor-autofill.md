# Session - 2026-11-22: the script box stops offering a contact's email (iPhone)

The owner's iPhone showed "AutoFill Contact" with email suggestions above the keyboard while writing in the script editor ("Why is it email?"). Cause: the editor's `<textarea>` declared nothing about itself (only `spellCheck={false}`), and iOS Safari treats an undeclared text box as a possible form field and offers the person's contact details. Fix: `components/editor/textHints.ts` (`SCRIPT_TEXT_HINTS`: `autoComplete="off"`, `autoCorrect="off"`, `spellCheck={false}`, and the `data-form-type` / `data-lpignore` / `data-1p-ignore` flags password managers read) on the script textarea (`WriteView`) and the beat-summary box on the outline cards (`EditorCenterViews`). Unit test pins the attributes. **Verified:** typecheck, the unit test. **Not verified: on a real iPhone.** `autocomplete="off"` is the documented way to say "not a form field", but iOS does not always honour it for the QuickType bar; if the AutoFill Contact bar still appears, the next step is a different `name`/`inputmode` on the field or moving the script into a `contenteditable`. Other editor boxes (find/replace, scene references) were left alone.
