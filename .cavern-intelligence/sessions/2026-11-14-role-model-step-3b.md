# Session - 2026-11-14: the role model, step 3b (an invitation can be answered; only an accepted one gives access)

**Found by looking first:** Recruit files a `project_crew` row as `pending`, and nothing could ever move it: only the project's creator may update a crew row, so an invitee had no way to accept or decline and every invitation stayed `pending` for ever. Worse, `can_access_project` counted any crew row whatever its status, so a person who had only been asked to join (or had declined) already read the project and its scripts. (Production is safe to tighten: every one of its 24 crew rows is `confirmed`.)

**Built** (migration `20261114000200`): `can_access_project` and `is_project_member` require `confirmed`; `my_crew_invitations()` (the caller's open invitations with the project's title, the craft and access offered, and who invited them: a pending invitee cannot read the project, so the title cannot come from a select); `respond_to_crew_invitation(project, accept)` (the invitee accepts or declines their own `pending` row, nothing else changes, the inviter is notified). The app has an **Invitations** card at the top of Community › Your day with Accept and Decline; accepting reloads the project list so the project appears. The owner can set a declined row back to `pending` to ask again.

**A second hole the persona test found while I wrote it:** I first changed only `can_access_project`. The test (an invitee reads nothing) still got the project row back, because the `projects` read policy goes through `is_project_member`, a different helper with the same flaw. Both now require `confirmed`; the same helper also sits under who-is-cast reads, script notes and channel creation.
