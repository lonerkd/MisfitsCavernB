# Session - 2026-11-18: music cue sheet and licensing; sound spotting

Two Production tools the role-suites audit named as gaps (R17): **Music cues & licensing** and **Sound spotting**. Cues: every piece of music with its cue number, title, kind (score, source, needle drop, vocal), how it is heard, composer, publisher, PRO, master owner, in and out timecodes (running time), licence status (needed, requested, negotiating, cleared, denied, original), fee, territory, term and contact; the header totals the music time, the agreed fees, the cues without a licence, the denied ones and the unpriced ones; each cue lists what still blocks it (no licence, no composer or publisher, no master owner for a needle drop, a bad time range, cleared without territory or term, a duplicate cue number); "Needs attention" filters to those. It **prints and exports as the cue sheet** (cue-number order with running times, `cueSheetRows`, not copied as typed). Spotting: scene, time, type (effect, foley, ambience, dialogue fix, ADR, walla), what is needed, source and status (to spot, sourced, cut, approved), with a progress bar. Both are linked to scenes, searchable, undoable and saved per record like the other role tools (`useSettingsList`).

Wired in: the Production tab (Departments), `PRODUCTION_VIEWS`, the Composer and Music supervisor suites (cues), the Sound designer and Re-recording mixer suites (spotting), print, change impact (Sound department), the pocket labels. The toolkit gap lines for them are gone.
