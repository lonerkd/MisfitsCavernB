# Session - 2026-11-15: the outside roles (BACKLOG 3.51 item 1, the second Thin role)

**The gap:** Client, Investor, Distributor or festival guest and Press had no surface but the client review link, and that link did the same for everyone: the database allowed an `investor` layer but nothing distinguished it, and the owner could not choose an audience when making a link. (The audit's "behind a flag" was stale: `guest_review` has been on in production since 2026-10-27.)

**Decided (mine, from the owner's "the other 2" after YouTube):** one audience per link. **Client** (`guest`) reads, comments and approves or asks for changes; **Investor** and **Distributor or festival** read and comment but cannot approve (approval is the client's); **Press** reads only. If the owner wants different rules, they live in two places: `lib/review/audiences.ts` and the two functions below.

**Built** (migration `20261115000400`): `review_links.layer` allows the four audiences; `decide_review` refuses any layer but the client's, `add_review_comment` refuses the press. Both are existing definer functions on the allowlist (`CREATE OR REPLACE` keeps their grants); existing links are all `guest` and behave as before. In the app: Studio › Share › Review makes a link for a chosen audience (the list says which); the public `/review/<token>` page shows only what the audience may do (no name box or buttons for the press, no Approve for an investor) and says why. `lib/review/audiences.ts` is the one list, and maps each outside craft to its audience.

**Tests:** persona `review-audiences.test.ts` (6: every audience reads through its link; investor, distributor and press cannot approve and nothing changes; investor and distributor can comment, the press cannot; the client still approves; an unknown audience, a non-shaper and an outsider are refused; a revoked link reads nothing) beside the existing 17; unit `audiences.test.ts`, `create-link.test.ts`; browser `e2e/review-audiences.spec.ts` (4, the public page for each audience). Not covered by a browser: the owner's audience picker in the Studio panel (typecheck and the unit test on `createLink` only).

**Not done (BACKLOG 3.51):** approval tied to a picture cut (media versions), an investor deck or budget summary, a screening link for a distributor, the press kit inside the link. **To do after merge:** apply `20261115000400` to production then staging, then the drift check.
