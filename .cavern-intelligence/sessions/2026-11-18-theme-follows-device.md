# Session - 2026-11-18: the default theme follows the device (owner decision)

The owner: "default is Misfit for me, but I know most apps are light-mode default". Asked which, they chose **follow the device**. A person who has never chosen a theme now gets **Paper on a light device and Misfit on a dark one**, and it changes with the device; any saved choice (the account's, or this device's) always wins; every theme stays one tap away.

**How.** `System` already existed (Paper in the light, but **Cavern** in the dark). `DEFAULT_CHOICE` is now `{ id: 'system' }` and `System`'s dark half is Misfit (`DEFAULT_THEME_ID` stays the brand look), so the default and the System card are the same thing and the card is the way back to it. The early script in `app/layout.tsx` (it paints before React) treats a missing, empty, broken or unknown stored value as System, so a light device never flashes dark; `ThemeInitializer` already followed the device while the choice is System. **A side effect to know about:** someone who had picked System before now gets Misfit, not Cavern, in the dark.

**A trap, closed.** A headless browser reports a *light* device, so with no saved choice the whole e2e suite (written against Misfit, and `accessibility.spec.ts` checks Misfit) would have silently started rendering Paper. `playwright.config.ts`, the theme scan and the phone audit are pinned to a dark device (`bible-shots` already was); `e2e/theme-default.spec.ts` covers the light one.

**Verified, against a rebuilt production build:** unit tests for the resolver, the early script (nothing stored, broken, unknown, stored-choice-wins, light and dark: 77 pass) and typecheck; `e2e/theme-default.spec.ts` 5 of 5 in a real browser (light gets Paper and the page is actually light, dark gets Misfit, it follows a live change of the device setting, a saved choice wins on either and survives a device change). **Not done:** the PWA's `theme_color` and `background_color` are static (Misfit's night, `#040710`), so a light device's status bar and splash stay dark until the manifest gets a light variant; not seen on a real iPhone.
