# 2026-11-26 — Every switch is the disc

The owner opened the editor's Tools panel and found two kinds of
on/off living side by side: wide pill switches for Typewriter mode
and Dark page, a round disc for Tag mode. "Diff buttons, make em
consistently the round ones. Globally."

The round one already existed — the DiscSwitch, written to be the
one switch for the whole suite. Most of the app had adopted it
(Settings, Studio, Lounge, the hub). Four places still drew their
own: the editor's ViewToggle (the pill), and Tag mode, Open all and
the Frosted theme switch, which were hand-copied discs in three
stylesheets — same geometry, three copies to drift.

All four now draw the one Lamp. The copies are deleted. Behaviour,
labels and keyboard handling are untouched; only the drawing is one
drawing now.

No database changes.
