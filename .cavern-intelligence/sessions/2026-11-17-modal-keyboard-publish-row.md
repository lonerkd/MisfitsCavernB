# Session - 2026-11-17: dialogs above the phone keyboard, and the clipped "Allow a case study" (BACKLOG 3.41)

**Seen in the 8-minute phone recording:** Library's Find references (and every Studio dialog) opened under the iPhone keyboard with the field being typed in out of sight, worst in landscape where the keyboard covers most of a 393 pt screen. Cause: the Studio `Modal` is a fixed, centred box sized to `100vh`, and iOS does not shrink the layout viewport for the keyboard.

**Changed (no migration):** while the keyboard is up (`usePhoneLayout().typing`) the `Modal`'s backdrop fits the visible part of the screen (`top: offset`, `height`), the dialog starts at the top and is at most that height minus 16 px, and the focused field is scrolled into view. Without a keyboard nothing changes. Unit-tested with the hook mocked (`components/studio/Modal.test.tsx`); **not seen with a real iOS keyboard**: headless Chromium has none, so a real iPhone pass in both orientations is still needed.

The base is two columns now, and the thumbnail rows opt in with `publishRowThumb`.
