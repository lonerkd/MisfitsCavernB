# Session - 2026-11-18: the Cavern experience, scrapped and rebuilt (BACKLOG 3.43)

**The owner:** "the cavern experience is whack and needs major scrapping the trash, and refining it to be our standards." Chose: rebuild straight away.

**What I found by looking at it in a browser** (desktop and phone): the Run view was a stick figure on thin orange outlines over a flat black; the Dive was grey translucent squares falling down a square shaft with a cursor ring, unrelated to a cave at all. Both read as a tech demo, not a place.

**Scrapped:** the first-person Dive and everything only it used (`lib/cavern/dive.ts`, `diveSound.ts`, `diveSteer.ts`, `components/shell/cavernDive.ts` and their tests), the Run / Dive switch, the Sound toggle and their two device settings (`mc_cavern_style`, `mc_cavern_sound`), the pointer-aim code that served it, and the `style` argument of `describeSync`. About 900 lines out.

**Rebuilt (`components/shell/cavernScene.ts`):** the cave is **painted as rock, not outlines**: four depths of filled rock in the brand's ink-blue (far to near, each darker, with fog between them), stalactites and stalagmites at places the song's seed picks (the same song has the same formations), a far cold light that breathes with the music, a beam of three soft wedges ahead of the lantern, the rock's rim and faces lit where the light reaches (the middle depth gets a share), a wet floor that catches the lantern's glow, dust, rock framing the corners that moves faster than the rest, a vignette and film grain. The figure is a **hooded silhouette** drawn as one filled shape (limbs, boots, cloak that streams with its speed, a lantern frame), lit from the front by its own lantern with a contact shadow. Palette follows the cover's colour for the light as before; the shadows are `#040710`.

**Caught on the way:** the first version drew the figure from several paths wound in opposite directions, so overlaps cancelled and left holes in it (visible in the first frame as a speckled body); every shape now goes through one `poly()` that winds consistently.

**Verified:** typecheck, lint, 83 unit tests (the scene with the song seed, the figure's silhouette and the lantern clipped to it, the visualizer, the journey text); **looked at in a browser** at 1440x900 and 393x852 on the dev server running the final code (frames compared with the old ones before and after each change). The earlier production build predates the last two passes (formations, soft beam, filled-limb fix): the dev server showed those. **Not verified:** a real iPhone, the corner window, listening to the music (the beat flare), the journey through a project's scenes with the new look; whether it meets the owner's standard is the owner's eye (the BACKLOG item says what is left).
