# Session - 2026-11-18: signable paperwork

Releases and contracts in Production › Paperwork can now be **sent for signature**. "Send for signature" on a release or contract row opens a starting text (crew deal memo, appearance release, location release, confidentiality agreement, `lib/studio/signing-templates.ts`) with the production, the person, the part, the rate and the dates filled in and `[blanks]` left visible; it is edited freely, then "Make the signing link" gives a link (`/sign/<token>`, two random uuids, works 30 days, no account). The signer reads the whole text, types their full name, ticks that they agree to sign electronically, and signs, or declines with a reason. The production keeps the name, the time and a SHA-256 of the exact text; the paperwork row turns to **Signed** (a request sent turns it to Sent); the owner is notified; a request can be withdrawn while open.

**Data:** `signing_requests` (migration `20261118000200`): the production's people (`internal.can_shape_project`) can read and create; no UPDATE or DELETE policy, so the text a person signed cannot be changed; a forged signed insert and a document from another project are refused by the insert check. The guest's three functions (`get_signing`, `sign_document`, `decline_signing`, anon-callable, exact token, 32+ characters) and `void_signing` (authenticated, `can_shape_project`) are on the allowlist in `database-and-security.md`.

**Verified:** typecheck, lint, unit tests (templates, the sign page's behaviour), `tests/integration/signing-requests.test.ts` (6 persona tests: guest read by exact token only, signs once with agreement and a real name, hash recorded, paperwork marked done and owner notified, decline, expiry, void by the production only, Riley and anon see nothing, no edit or delete, forgeries refused). **Not verified:** the Paperwork UI and the sign page in a browser; no e2e. **Migration:** apply `20261118000200` to production, then staging, then the drift check, after merge.
