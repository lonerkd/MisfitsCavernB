# Session - 2026-11-18: a personal calendar feed

Settings › Calendar makes one secret link per person (`calendar_feeds`, owner-only RLS) that Apple, Google or Outlook calendars subscribe to: shoot days with a date, **issued** call sheets (the person's own call time from `call_sheet_calls`, else the general call, ending at the estimated wrap) and open tasks assigned to them, on projects they created or are confirmed crew on. The link can be copied, opened as `webcal:`, replaced (the old one stops) or switched off. Read with no login through `calendar_feed(token)` (SECURITY DEFINER, on the allowlist) by `/api/calendar/<token>.ics` (`lib/calendar/ics.ts` builds the file: floating local times, all-day dates, escaping and 75-octet folding). An unknown token gets an empty calendar, so tokens cannot be probed.

**Verified:** typecheck, lint, `lib/calendar` unit tests; `tests/integration/calendar-feed.test.ts` against the local stack (exact token, no login, own call beats the general call, drafts are plain days, nobody sees another person's project or tasks, revoked and switched-off tokens stop, Riley and anon cannot read or forge a token row). Not tried in a real calendar app, and the route and Settings card were not run in a browser. **Migration `20261118000100`:** apply to production, then staging, then the drift check, after merge. **Left:** other deadline kinds (delivery dates, permits expiring), a per-project feed for people with no account.
