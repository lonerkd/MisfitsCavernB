# Session - 2026-11-18: the flaky specs, reproduced and sorted (BACKLOG 3.37)

A red nightly people learn to ignore is the same problem as no nightly (see `2026-11-18-quality-gates.md`), so the seven specs that failed now and then were run **together under load** (the way the nightly runs them), three runs and then six more, and each failure read before anything was changed.

**1. `accessibility` was not flaky, it was red on every run: a real defect.** On `/` the notification bell shows an unread number but its `aria-label` was only "Notifications": the visible text was not part of its accessible name (axe `label-content-name-mismatch`, WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name; a screen-reader user hears "Notifications" while a speech-control user says "3"). It only fails while something is unread, which is why it looked intermittent. The name is now "Notifications, 3 unread" ("9+" past nine), with `NotificationBell.test.tsx` (fails without the fix: checked). Rebuilt and ran: **passes**, after failing 6 of 6 on the build without it.
**2. `admin-support-analytics`:** the page shows the answer as soon as it is sent; the spec then read the case's status and the member's notification at once, before they were written. It waits for them now (`expect.poll`).
**3. `script-supervisor`:** a scene picked just before the view finished loading was lost, and no wait on the link could bring it back (it failed again with a 30 s wait). The spec retries the pick until the link shows, as a person would. Whether the view itself should keep a pick made while it loads is an open product question, in 3.37.
**Verified:** six runs under load of the fixed specs together: `script-supervisor` and `admin-support-analytics` 6 of 6; `accessibility` after a rebuild. **Not fixed, said plainly:** `editor-cowriting` and `editor-offline-merge` passed 6 of 6 in this load, so there is no reproduction and nothing was changed to make them pass; they failed twice earlier under heavier load, and the way the sync works (best-effort broadcasts, no periodic repair after the join) means a dropped message would leave two windows different until a reload. That needs a probe recording each window and the database at the moment it fails, not a longer wait. `mobile` and `phases` did not fail in any sweep run.
