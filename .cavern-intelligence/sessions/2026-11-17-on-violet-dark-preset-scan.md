# Session - 2026-11-17: the dark presets scanned; white on pale violet fixed (BACKLOG 3.35)

`npm run theme:scan` over the default routes in the five dark presets not yet scanned (Vampire, Terminal, Mono, Neon, Forest) on the local stack and a production build, as the demo account. All five reported the **same single defect**, none other: on `/jobs` the "Post Job" button was white (`#fff`) on `--violet`, which is the pale `#a5b4fc` in dark themes (1.99:1). Paper and the other light themes were fine because their `--violet` is the deep `#4338ca`. The job modal's submit button had the same fault the other way round (`--fg-strong` on the violet fill: white in dark, dark ink in light, so wrong on both; not on a scanned route).

**Fix:** a token, `--on-violet` (`#0b0e1a` on the dark themes' violet, `#fff` on the three light themes' and in the custom-theme generator in `lib/themes/themes.ts`), used by both buttons. Re-scanned `/jobs` after a rebuild in Neon, Paper and Misfit: every piece of text reads. Misfit (Ink & Rust) had already scanned clean on its routes. Not checked: the modal's submit button by eye or by scan (it opens on a click; the scan sees routes), other `--violet` fills elsewhere (none found by search with a hard-coded white).
