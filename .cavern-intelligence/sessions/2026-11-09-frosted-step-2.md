# Session — 2026-11-09: frosted mode, step 2 (the rest of the surfaces)

**Built:** the blur now reaches the surfaces step 1 left solid. Eleven CSS-module rules that paint with `--glass` / `--surface` and did not blur (the island, the split bar and its pane menu, the brief and guide panels, Your day, the mood-board cards, the shot and cut marks) take `backdrop-filter: var(--frost, none)`. Three attribute rules in `app/globals.css` reach what is painted inline: `--glass` / `--surface` fills, and a rounded box filled with the solid `--bg-2` / `--bg-3` (Settings' sections and most cards on the pages), which becomes the same lit glass; that last rule sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: no-preference)` and `@supports (backdrop-filter)` so a person who asked for less transparency, or a browser without the blur, keeps the opaque card instead of a translucent one with nothing behind it. A unit test checks the three rules are in the layer.

**Found by looking, and fixed:** (1) on Paper the tool header went muddy taupe, because the "lit by the text colour" tint darkens a light theme. The lift is now a pair of variables, `--frost-tint` / `--frost-lift`: the text colour at 8% on a dark theme, **white at 44% on a light one** (`paper`, `editorial`, `lagoon`, and a custom theme by its own lightness in `customTokens`); a test keeps the light list in the CSS equal to the light themes in the catalogue, and the contrast test models each mode. (2) Settings' cards were translucent with the blur on but looked unchanged over a flat dark page: two soft mid-page washes were added so glass has colour to catch as the page scrolls.

**Looked at on screen** (a production build against the local stack): Slate, Paper and Cavern, off and on, on Settings, the Studio's Locations and the project page; Paper reads as frosted white, Slate and Cavern as lit glass over coloured washes. **Not verified:** `npm run theme:scan` with the mode on; the blur's cost on the stripboard and the editor; surfaces painted from a CSS class with a solid `--bg-2` rather than inline.
