# Session — 2026-11-08: why `e2e/breakdown.spec.ts` failed on `main`

The spec waited for the button "Tag Lantern as Props" and never found it, on plain `main`. **Cause (not a product bug):** since #423 `suggestForScene` offers no capitalised-word suggestions when the scene's read is doubtful, and a scene's confidence is the minimum over its lines (`lib/breakdown/marks.ts`). The spec's script had its only character, SAM, speak once, and the reader scores a cue seen once at 60 (below the 70 cut-off), so scene 1 counted as doubtful and LANTERN was never offered. A probe over the parser showed it: one speech, cue 60; two speeches, 100. **Fix:** SAM speaks twice in the spec's script (with a comment saying why). **Verified:** the spec passes against a fresh build on the local stack (it failed identically on `main` before). **Recorded for the owner (BACKLOG 3.42):** whether the doubtful-read rule should look only at the scene's action lines, since one first-time cue silences a whole scene's suggestions.
