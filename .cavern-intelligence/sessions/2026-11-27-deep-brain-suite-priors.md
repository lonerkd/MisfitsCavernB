# Session - 2026-11-27: the deep brain, the suite's context as priors (BACKLOG 3.21)

The owner's next rung (STATE 2e item 5): the script reader uses what the rest of the suite already knows as evidence, deterministically and explainably, under a person's own corrections. Pure TypeScript plus one data loader; **no migration** (every table it reads exists, with RLS that lets the owner and the crew read it alike).

**Built**
- **`lib/scriptos/suite-priors.ts`**: which rows are a person's evidence (`suitePriorsFromRows`) and the merge under a person's word (`withSuitePriors`). Evidence: this script's character bible entries a person wrote something in or gave a look-board (an empty entry is the editor's, not evidence), a bible full name as a believed alias; breakdown elements in Cast (a character) and in object categories (a thing: props, wardrobe, hair and makeup, set dressing, vehicles and animals, effects, sound, equipment; extras, stunts and custom categories say nothing); roles with casting candidates (a character). The cast and the confirmed names are recorded as sources too (`contextFromProject`), so every explanation can say where a name came from.
- **Parser and decoder**: a suite name is believed like a casting; a thing costs a cue (`knownThing` 16) and, for a line that is only its name, speech (`knownThingSpeech` 10); the pre-scan no longer counts a thing as a speaker on its shape; a thing that heads speech three times or more is read as a speaker after all, capped at 70 and offered back (`suiteThingOutweighedHeads`, `suiteDisagreeConfidenceCap`). Matching ignores a leading article ("THE BRIEFCASE" is the element "Briefcase").
- **A person's word wins**: a rejected name loses every suite source (shown as overruled); a cast or confirmed name is never a thing; a line override is fixed before any prior. No prior touches a heading, so the scene index never moves.
- **Loaded in all three places that read with a context**: `studio.listSuitePriorRows` (lib/studio/api.ts), the `useSuitePriors` hook in the editor and the Studio's scene sync (reloads when the page comes back into view), and `loadReadingContext` for sides, the lined script, the Cast tab and the scene timeline.
- **Shown where the reader explains itself**: line reasoning (`Known to the project: in the cast, in the character bible`; `The breakdown lists BRIEFCASE as Props: not read as a speaker`), the doubt's explanation (`review.ts suiteNote`), and a new **From the project** section in the Script tab's *What the reader knows*: the script's format and the rules it is read with, the names believed and from where with cue counts, lines held back with *It's a character*, disagreements with *A character* / *Not a character* (each saves the project-wide `reading_corrections` verdict, toast on success and on failure), and what a person's word overruled.

**Decided (not owner calls, recorded here):** crew are not a prior (a real name says nothing about a script's lines and must not show up in the reader's explanations); the brief is not a prior (its answers carry no evidence about how a line is written; the format already picks the grammar, and is now shown); the `characters` / `scene_characters` rows stay out (the sync writes them from the reader); the bible is read for this script only (a crew member may open one script and not another, and the shared scene index must read the same for everyone who syncs it).
