# Session — 2026-11-07: the phone experience, slice 1 — the editor

The owner tests on an iPhone 15 Pro and said the phone UI is a squeezed desk layout with no logic of its own. `scripts/mobile-audit.mjs` (new) walks every route at 393×852 and 852×393 and reports sideways overflow, small targets, controls under bars and whether the focused field survives the keyboard; the editor was the worst (a header that scrolled sideways in silence, a desk drawer, three columns in 100 pt of landscape). Built: `usePhoneLayout` (phone, landscape, keyboard height, visible-area offset; `visualViewport`, since iOS never resizes the layout viewport for the keyboard), `BottomSheet`, and the editor's phone mode: `PhoneEditorBar` (back, script, view, tools, more), sheets for scripts and scenes, view, tools (the whole right panel) and more (save, find, character bible, focus, export, share), `PhoneElementBar` (Scene/Action/Character/Dialogue/Transition/Note over the keyboard, pointerdown cancelled so the keyboard stays), the page fitting the visible area while typing, the island and table-read button stepping aside (`html[data-typing]`), the bar and status line gone in landscape typing. **Verified:** typecheck, lint, 14 unit tests, and screenshots of the built app at both sizes with a faked keyboard. **Not verified:** a real iPhone (Safari's toolbar, safe areas, the real keyboard). **Left:** BACKLOG 3.41 (plan in `plans/2026-11-07-phone-experience.md`).
