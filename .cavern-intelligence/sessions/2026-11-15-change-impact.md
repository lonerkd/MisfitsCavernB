# Session - 2026-11-15: a director's change reaches the departments (BACKLOG 3.51 item 3, X2)

**The gap:** the Director notebook held notes, but nothing carried a change to the people it affected. A director who moved a scene to night had to remember who in Art, Wardrobe, Camera and Production to tell. The audit's X2: "nothing carries a director's change to a department".

**Built (no migration; real notifications through the existing table):** a Director notebook note can be marked **Changes the plan**. Once it is linked to scenes, **Who does this touch?** works it out from what is really linked to those scenes: the breakdown elements tagged to them (by category: props and set dressing to Art, wardrobe and makeup to Wardrobe, hair & makeup, sound, VFX, camera equipment, and so on), the role tools' records (costumes, makeup, VFX shots, camera and sound reports, stills, extras, script notes), the planned shots (Camera), and the shoot day (Production and Direction). It lists each department with the reasons and the confirmed crew in it (a department with no one yet says so), plus anyone a touched breakdown element is assigned to. **Tell N people** asks to confirm, then notifies each of them with the change, its scenes and what it means for them; the note then shows who was told and when, and offers "Tell them again" (with a confirm). The sender is never told their own change; a pending or declined invitee is never told (they cannot open the project); the owner is told only of an element assigned to them. A failure to notify some is said plainly, not hidden.

**Tests:** unit `change-impact.test.ts` (7; it caught a real flaw while it was being written: an element assigned to someone who is not confirmed crew would have notified them); browser `e2e/change-impact.spec.ts` (the department, reasons and people shown; axe clean; Tell sends a notification to exactly the costume designer and the DP and not the sound crew or the sender; the note shows who was told). **Not done:** raising it automatically from a script revision; each person's acknowledgement.
