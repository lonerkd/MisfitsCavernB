# Session - 2026-11-14: the role model, step 4 (the invite sentence and a role on Your day)

**The gap:** step 1 let the owner choose a craft and an access level, step 2 and 3 made them mean something, but nobody could be told what they meant: Recruit showed two pickers and the invitation showed a title, so a person accepted without knowing what they would and would not see.

**Built (no migration):** `describeRole` in `lib/roles/core.ts` says it in words from a craft, an access level and a capability set. It names the three kinds of information that are not shared by default (the budget, casting submissions, paperwork) as *can* or *cannot*, lists what the craft adds (write the script, issue call sheets…) in catalogue order, and tells a view-only member they can read and not change. `RoleSentence` shows it live in Recruit under the access choice, and on the invitation card before the person accepts (for someone not yet on the project it resolves the rights from the craft's template with the tier as the ceiling, as the database does). A new **Your role** card on Community › Your day lists each project you are crew on (not the ones you run) with the sentence from the database's own answer (`my_capabilities`: it includes the owner's per-person changes and extra crafts) and shortcuts to only the tools the role holds (`toolsFor`).
