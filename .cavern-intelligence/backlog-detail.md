### 3.48 Frosted mode for every theme — M (owner, 2026-11-09: "Slate's glass mode is sexy, might gotta make a frosted mode for each of them")

Under it each theme's own surface tokens (`--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--glass`, the panel and card backgrounds) become translucent mixes of that theme's `--bg-2` / `--bg-3` (`color-mix`, never a hard-coded colour), the surface classes (`.glass`, `.panel`, the island, sheets, menus, the new `ToolHeader`) get `backdrop-filter: blur() saturate()`, a hairline light border and a faint top highlight, and the page behind them carries enough ambient colour (the existing `--ambient-r/g/b` glow, a soft gradient) for the blur to show. **The cost to respect:** text on a translucent surface must still pass the contrast floor on every theme (the tokens test, `npm run theme:scan`), `prefers-reduced-transparency` and a device that cannot blur fall back to the opaque surfaces, and the blur is kept off the heaviest views (the stripboard, the editor's text area). **Data:** the choice belongs to the account (`profiles.ui_prefs.frosted`, validated by `set_my_ui_prefs`: a migration and a persona-style test) and is copied to the device so the first paint is right, as the theme is. **Step 1 built 2026-11-09:** the switch in Settings › Appearance, `theme.frosted` in the account (migration `20261109000000`: apply to production then staging), the early-paint script, the token layer for every theme at once, and `--frost` on `.glass*`, the Studio's panels, the phase panel and `ToolHeader`; ### 3.26 Story-structure templates — S/M (owner idea, 2026-10-20)
