# Brand

**The Cavern** is the product; **Misfits Cavern** is the company that makes it.
Decisions and the plan: [`../restructure-proposal.md`](../restructure-proposal.md) › Decisions.

- `concepts/` — the mark, three candidates (owner to pick), 64×64 SVG in
  the house colours (night ink `#040710`, vanilla `#e0ddae`, sinopia
  `#e8431a`):
  - `peaks.svg` — A: two peaks make the M; lit faces vanilla, faces turned
    from the light in sinopia, a cast shadow.
  - `moon.svg` — B: a crescent opening right makes the C; one sinopia star.
  - `moon-peaks.svg` — C: the crescent with the lit peaks inside it.
  - `sheet.png` — all three on the Cavern and Paper themes, as an iPhone
    icon, at 32/16 px and in the landing lockup. Rebuild:
    `MC_LOCAL_CHROMIUM=<chrome path, optional> node scripts/brand-sheet.mjs`.
- Voice: "Welcome back, misfit." stays.
- Landing: "THE CAVERN", "by Misfits Cavern" beneath.
