# One home (2026-11-18)

Owner: Community and the front page were a confusing number of different ways to the same things, some useless, some good, some right for the public and some for after sign-in. One universal page, the 3D logo on top, and every state and device decided. The owner's favourite piece: the part previews that show a design when signed out and your own project's real content when signed in. The earlier versions of those (the front page's grid, the Community page's picker, the resume band) are now one component each for the two layouts.

## What was there

- **Front page `/`**: the 3D mark, hero, pipeline, numbers, a ticker, one tile per part of the suite with a preview (a demo signed out, your latest script, references and messages signed in), a closing invitation; a "resume" band for signed-in people.
- **Community `/community`** (signed-in only): your day, what's new, crew open to work, **The Cavern** (the same numbers plus a tabbed one-at-a-time picker of the same four previews), house music.
- Plus: a "Front page" entry in the island, taskbar and phone menu; "Community" in the top bar, taskbar, tab bar and palette; `/today` and `/hub` redirects to Community.

**Gone:** the Community page as a place (its address redirects with the query string, so the phone's share sheet and old bookmarks work); the "The Cavern" block (its numbers and picker live on the page itself now); the resume band; the "Front page" entries in the island, taskbar and phone menu; the "Community" top-bar link (the logo is Home).

**Rule for what changes after sign-in:** the pitch goes (pipeline, numbers, the invitation); the work arrives (your day, what's new, your parts with your content). Nothing a guest needs is behind a login; nothing a signed-in person needs is below a pitch. A part with no work of yours yet still shows its demo design, so an empty account never sees a blank tile.

**Device rule:** the desktop shows all four previews at once because there is room; a phone shows one at a time because four tall previews in a column is a scroll to nowhere. The phone is detected by the suite's own `usePhoneLayout`, not by width alone.
